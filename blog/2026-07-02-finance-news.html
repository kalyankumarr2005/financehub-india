RBI Updates: Rupee's Persistent Weakness

Despite the broader market's cheerful mood, the Indian rupee faced continued headwinds today, extending its losing streak for the fourth consecutive session against the US dollar. The currency's performance was notably challenging, as it weakened even amidst a generally weaker dollar globally and despite signs of likely intervention from the central bank. The rupee opened strong, touching an early peak of 94.9375, but quickly reversed course.

Market flows, particularly those related to arbitrage and merchant payments, exerted significant pressure on the currency. Traders noted an increased dollar demand, which triggered stop-losses on rupee bets as it crossed the crucial 95 mark. The initial bounce fizzled out as the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) intervention appeared to thin out, and dollar offers from state-run banks faded. The rupee eventually settled flat, last trading at 95.2775. This persistent weakness, despite central bank efforts and a broader dollar slump, highlights underlying demand-supply imbalances and speculative pressures in the foreign exchange market. Investors will be keenly watching the rupee's trajectory, especially with global cues like the upcoming U.S. non-farm payrolls report potentially influencing currency movements.
